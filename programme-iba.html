Parliamentary service commissions and new or revived parliamentary institutes
Duration
Six to twelve months
Certification
Not applicable
CPD hours
Not applicable
Fee
USD 60,000–200,000 per engagement
How to join
Request from a parliamentary service commission

Offerings

Offered on request. A parliament or development partner asks for an engagement; the programme director agrees the scope, dates and fee.

Courses

Each course has an approved course book
IBA101Designing a Parliamentary Training Institute

Apply or nominate

Individuals apply online. Parliaments, ministries and partners nominate participants and receive an invoice; online payment by card or mobile money follows.

Certificates

Certificates show the programme, the CPD hours and a verification code that anyone can check on the verification page.
