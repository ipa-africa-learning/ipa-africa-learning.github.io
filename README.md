# IPA Africa Learning: clickable prototype

A clickable prototype of the learning platform of the **Institute of Parliamentary Affairs Africa (IPA Africa)**: a
programme catalogue with dated offerings, participant and staff dashboards, course books (scroll and flip-book reader),
quizzes, timed assessments, practice questions, an AI study assistant, course discussions, cohort networks, certificates
with public verification, and live sessions (online classes) with attendance and recordings.

**Open the prototype:** https://ipa-africa-learning.github.io/

## How to try it

- On the sign-in page, choose one of the demo accounts under **"or try a demo account"**: participant, alumna,
  participant in an upcoming offering, facilitator, programme director, sponsor liaison, faculty author, helpdesk
  officer or administrator. No password is needed.
- Use **Screens** and **Next** in the bar at the bottom of every page to walk through the main screens in order.
- Everything you do is kept in your own browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. **My profile > Reset demo data**
  starts again.

## Please note

- This is a concept prototype, not a live service. The production platform runs on Moodle.
- All people and accounts are fictional; organisations are named only to illustrate. Dates, venues and fees are
  illustrative planning figures.
- The live classroom is a simulation: it does not use your camera or microphone. On the production platform, joining a
  live session opens the actual Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom or BigBlueButton meeting.

Feedback is welcome through the IPA Africa team that shared this link with you.
