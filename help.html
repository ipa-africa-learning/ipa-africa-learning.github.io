Help and support

?How do I join a programme?
Choose an offering on the Programmes page and apply, or ask your parliament, ministry or sponsor to nominate you. Sponsored places are invoiced to the sponsor; individuals pay by card or mobile money.
?How do I read my course books offline?
Open the course and choose Download. In the Moodle app, books and audio are small; download recordings on Wi-Fi where possible. Your notes sync when you reconnect.
?What is the difference between the scroll view and the flip-book view?
Both show the same book with the same page numbers. The scroll view is best on phones; the flip-book view turns pages like a printed book. Switch with the Scroll and Flip buttons at the top of the reader.
?Can I print my course book?
Yes. During your programme you may print a personal study copy, up to two copies per book. Every page carries your name and participant ID and a copy ID. Reproduction or sale is prohibited.
?What happens to my books after the programme?
As an alumnus you keep read-only online access for twelve months, with your bookmarks and notes. Downloading, printing and the AI assistant are not available in that period.
?How do certificates work?
When you complete a programme you receive a certificate with your CPD hours and a verification code. Employers and sponsors can check the code on the verification page.
?How do live sessions work?
Your facilitators hold live sessions on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom or BigBlueButton. They appear on your Dashboard and under Live sessions on the course page. Join through the platform: the Join button opens 15 minutes before the start and records your attendance. When your facilitator shows a check-in code, enter it. You are told before you join if a session is recorded; recordings stay available to your offering, including as alumni. Attendance can count towards your certificate.
?What can my sponsor see?
Your sponsor sees your enrolment, completion and certificate. It does not see your notes, discussions, cohort-network content or individual marks.
?What are cohort networks?
Private workspaces you create with fellow participants: invite them, chat, keep shared notes, share resources and plan meetings. Facilitators do not see them. Spaces for the Speakers' programme follow the Chatham House rule.
?What is the AI study assistant?
A service included for participants that answers questions from your approved course books and practice questions, and shows the unit, section and page it used. It is not for assessed work and is switched off during assessments.

Contact the helpdesk

Every request gets a reference number so you can follow it until it is resolved.

Data protection

IPA Africa is registered with the Data Protection Commission as a data controller. Read how your data are used, and contact the data protection supervisor.

Report a serious concern anonymously

Reports go to an independent unit through a separate, secure system. You receive a receipt number to follow up without revealing who you are. Nothing about your report is recorded in IPA Africa Learning.
