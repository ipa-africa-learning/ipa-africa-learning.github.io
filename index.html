CONCEPT PROTOTYPE · OCTOBER 2026

IPA Africa Learning

A working prototype of the learning platform of the Institute of Parliamentary Affairs Africa: executive education for parliaments and public institutions, with the fifteen programmes of the Institute's strategy.

Sign in Programmes and calendar
Concept prototype, not a live service. Programmes follow the IPA Africa strategy (October 2026). Dates, venues and fees are illustrative. Course-book text, questions and people are fictional samples; organisations are named only to illustrate. Everything you do is saved only in this browser, so several accounts can be tried on one device. Use Screens and Next at the bottom of every page to move around.

Explore the features

Six groups; each link opens a working screen

Learn

Course books in a scroll view and a flip-book view with the same page numbers: bookmarks, highlights, notes, search, read-aloud, text size and night mode. Alumni keep read-only access for twelve months.

  • Participant dashboard
  • Course page
  • Reader: scroll view
  • Reader: flip-book view
  • Alumni access
  • Mobile app

Practise and assess

Self-checks for every course with explanations and best scores; timed assessments with one attempt, automatic submission and an integrity log; practice questions with marking guides.

  • Assessments
  • Quizzes
  • Take a quiz
  • Practice questions

Meet live

Online classes on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom or BigBlueButton: participants join through the platform and check in with a code; facilitators keep the attendance register; recordings stay available.

  • Live sessions
  • A live session
  • Live classroom (simulation)
  • Session recording

Work together

Cohort networks that participants build themselves (chat, shared notes, resources and meetings), course discussions moderated by facilitators, and an AI study assistant that cites unit, section and page.

  • Cohort networks
  • Course discussion
  • AI study assistant

Certify

Certificates with CPD hours and a verification code that employers and sponsors can check on a public page, without seeing marks or other personal data.

  • My certificates
  • Verify a certificate

Run the programme

Fifteen programmes with dated offerings, venues and fees, and staff homes for facilitators, programme directors, sponsor liaisons, the helpdesk and administrators, with reports. 208 demo participants from 14 countries and 67 staff accounts.

  • Programmes and calendar
  • Staff home
  • Reports
  • Demo accounts
