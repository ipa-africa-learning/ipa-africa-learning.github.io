Twelve-week blended certificate, or a five-day residential programme6 courses46 enrolled · 0 alumni (demo)

Members and chairs of public accounts, finance and budget committees; committee clerks and researchers; budget office and audit staff
Duration
Twelve weeks blended, with two residential weeks
Certification
IPA Africa executive certificate; university-accredited from year two or three
CPD hours
60
Fee
USD 2,500–4,000 per participant
How to join
Open enrolment, sponsored places and institutional nominations
